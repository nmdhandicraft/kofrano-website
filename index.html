Brand Policies

NMD guarantees the highest quality standards, seamless transport support, and absolute client protection.

5-Year Global Warranty

NMD luggage is engineered to perform. Our 5-Year Global Warranty covers all structural defects, handle malfunctions, shell fractures, and wheel failures. If it breaks during transit, NMD will repair or replace it at any major European airport terminal hub.

100-Day Travel Trial

Pack it, roll it, and board with it. We offer a 100-day trial period. If the NMD KOFRANO doesn't completely redefine the way you travel, return it for a full refund. No questions asked. We cover all domestic return shipping costs.

Carbon-Neutral Transit

Under our NMDTrendz.com commitment, every shipment is carbon-neutral. We offset 100% of the emissions generated from our manufacturing plant in Germany to your doorstep. The KOFRANO interior lining is made from 100% recycled PET ocean plastics.
